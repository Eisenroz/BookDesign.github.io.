About This Project

This project is a showcase of Egyptian artifacts displayed at the MET museum. The project explores different eras of Egypt and present the corresponding remnants of each era.

The goal was to create a magazine like book that both inform readers about the different eras of Egypt, through brief overviews of the timelines and its artifacts. The book acts as a sneak peak to the MET museum, and to replicate the awesome and educational nature when going to a museum.
